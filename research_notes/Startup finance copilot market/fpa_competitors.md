# Startup/SMB FP&A, forecasting and runway tools: competitive landscape (as of Nov 2026)

Scope note: the research covered about 25 web searches/fetches. Most pricing and rating figures come from third-party buyer-guide or competitor blogs (CFO Shortlist, Eightx, Metapraxis, Datarails, Capterra). Treat those as estimates. Where a primary source (press release or company blog) was found, it is cited. Many funding figures are 2022–2023 vintage. This is flagged inline.

## Q1. Competitor-by-competitor fact base (segment, features, AI, pricing, funding, M&A, integrations, reputation)

### Takeaway
The original "founder-friendly FP&A" cohort from 2020–2022 has mostly been absorbed or has moved up-market. Pry went to Brex (2022). Finmark went to BILL (2022) and was sunset on 1 Apr 2026. Causal went to Lucanet (Oct 2024). Mosaic went to HiBob (Feb 2025). Fathom went to Access Group (2022). The independents still standing (Runway, Cube, Abacum, Drivetrain, Jirav, Vareto, Datarails) now sell to finance teams at roughly $1M–$100M+ ARR, quote-only, around $10K–$80K/yr. Self-serve tools for seed-stage founders are left to low-priced modelers (LivePlan, Brixx, Sturppy, Forecastr) and cash-flow apps (Float, Fathom).

### Inferences
- Every 2020–2022 "Excel replacement for founders" product with traction (Pry, Finmark, Causal, Mosaic) was bought by a platform: spend (Brex), AP/AR (BILL), CFO suite (Lucanet) or HR (HiBob). ## Q2. Synthesis: crowded vs underserved segments, the up-market and acquisition pattern, lessons for a new entrant

### Takeaway
The mid-market FP&A segment (finance teams at $5M–$100M ARR) is crowded with well-funded AI-repositioned players. The seed–Series A founder without a finance hire is underserved, now more so: Pry, Finmark and Causal-as-startup-tool have been absorbed, and Finmark users were displaced in April 2026. A new entrant can win there with self-serve, flat pricing, accounting/bank/Stripe integrations and a trustworthy AI copilot. But it must plan for the fact that this segment historically monetised poorly and exited by acquisition into spend, AP or HR platforms.

### Cited Findings
- Mid-market crowding: Abacum $60M (Jun 2025), Datarails $70M (Jan 2026, total $175M), Cube $30M+$20M, Drivetrain $15M, Jirav $33M total and Runway ~$33M, all targeting finance teams — [Scale VP](https://www.scalevp.com/press/exclusive--abacum-lands--60m-to-bring-ai-to-business-planning); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/datarails-raises-70m-series-c-led-by-one-peak-to-make-ai-the-foundation-of-the-cfos-office-302666573.html); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/15/cube-which-wants-to-help-finance-teams-plan-better-and-faster-lands-30m-after-seeing-a-400-growth-in-arr)
- Runway's own best fit is 50–500 employees and $5M–$50M ARR, above FinanceOps' 2–50 person target — [CFO Shortlist](https://www.cfoshortlist.com/vendors/runway)
- Pattern of spend/finance platforms buying founder FP&A: Brex–Pry ($90M, Apr 2022), BILL–Finmark (Nov 2022, then sunset Apr 2026), Access–Fathom (Aug 2022), Lucanet–Causal (Oct 2024), HiBob–Mosaic (~$35M, Feb 2025) — sources above.
- The post-sunset BILL successor is QuickBooks-only, capped at 13 months, and only sold bundled with BILL. That leaves ex-Finmark founders with a narrower tool — [Finloom](https://www.finloom.io/blog/finmark-shut-down-alternative.html); [Metapraxis](https://metapraxis.com/finmark-alternatives)
- The low end is fragmented and cheap: LivePlan $15–$40/mo, Brixx ~£30/mo, Sturppy on AppSumo lifetime deals, Float limited to Xero/QBO/FreeAgent — [Startup Savant](https://startupsavant.com/liveplan-review); [Capterra Brixx](https://www.capterra.com/p/10009361/Brixx/); [AppSumo](https://appsumo.com/products/sturppy/); [Capterra Float](https://www.capterra.com/p/226486/Float-Cash-Flow/)
- Mosaic's price (~$35M after a $25M Series B) and Baremetrics' ~$4M sale suggest modest exit values for SMB-oriented finance tooling — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/ryvodwoyjl); [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/us-fintech-mosaic-secures-25m-series-b-funding); [Baremetrics](https://baremetrics.com/blog/i-sold-baremetrics)

### Inferences
- **Crowded:** mid-market AI FP&A (Runway, Abacum, Cube, Datarails, Drivetrain, Vareto, Planful), and accountant-channel reporting and forecasting (Fathom, Jirav, Float).
- **Underserved:** pre-finance-hire seed–Series A founders who want a live runway, burn, AR and scenario answer tied to bank, accounting and Stripe data at a self-serve price. They want more than a static business plan (LivePlan) and less than a $30K mid-market platform. Displaced Finmark users are an immediate, addressable pool.
- **Why the pattern happens:** small startups have low ACVs and churn as they die or graduate. Products either move up-market to finance teams (Runway, Cube, Abacum) or get bought by a platform that already owns the founder's money flow (card, AP, HR) and treats planning as a retention feature.
- **Lessons for FinanceOps:**
  1. Pick a side deliberately. Either be self-serve and cheap with a clear graduate path (export to Runway/Abacum-class tools), or plan from day one for distribution through banks, spend platforms or accountants. Acquirers and partners are those platforms.
  2. Integrations are table stakes: QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, Plaid-style bank feeds, Gusto/payroll. Float's Xero/QBO-only scope and BILL's QBO-only successor show how integration limits strand users.
  3. AI is now a commodity claim. Differentiate on trustworthy, sourced answers from the company's own data and on founder-language explanations (runway, default alive), not on "AI-native" labels.
  4. Data portability matters. The Finmark shutdown cost users who had not exported their models, so export and continuity guarantees are a trust lever.
  5. Flat or unlimited-seat pricing is already a differentiator upstream (Runway). At the low end, transparent pricing beats quote-only.

### Gaps
- No quantitative market sizing (number of seed–Series A startups using any FP&A tool, willingness to pay) was found.
- Reddit/HN founder sentiment on these tools was not collected systematically. Only the Causal HN thread was found.
- Brex/Ramp/Mercury's native forecasting features are out of scope (another researcher covers them). They matter for the "underserved" claim, because those platforms may already serve the low end.
